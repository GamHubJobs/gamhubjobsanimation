Privacy Policy

GamHub Jobs is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored, and your rights as a user operating in The Gambia and beyond.

Section 01

Who We Are

GamHub Jobs is a career platform designed for professionals in The Gambia. We help job seekers build professional CVs and cover letters, search and apply for jobs, and connect with employers across all industries in The Gambia.

We operate under Gambian law. Our data infrastructure uses Supabase, with servers located in the European Union (West EU region), subject to GDPR-equivalent data protection standards.

✉

Data Controller

gamhubjobs@gmail.com
Section 02

What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

Account and Identity Data

  • Email address (used as your account identifier via Supabase Auth magic link)
  • Name and professional title (provided voluntarily when building your CV)

CV and Professional Data

  • Full name, contact details (phone number, city, country)
  • Work history: job titles, employers, dates, and role descriptions
  • Educational background: institutions, qualifications, and years
  • Skills, certifications, languages, and professional achievements
  • Professional summary and cover letter content
  • References (names and contact details you choose to provide)

Employer Data

  • Company name, industry, and contact email
  • Job listing content including title, description, location, salary, and deadline

Usage Data

  • Saved job listings (stored in your browser's local storage)
  • Design preferences: colour palette, font choice, and CV theme (stored locally)
Section 03

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the service: generating your CV, cover letter, and job matches
  • To authenticate you: managing your login session securely via Supabase Auth
  • To improve the platform: understanding which features are most used (we do not sell this data)
  • To publish job listings: displaying employer-submitted job posts to job seekers after admin review
We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your CV content, email address, or any personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
Section 04

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in two places:

  • Supabase (cloud database): Your account profile, email address, and submitted job listings are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase in the European Union (West EU region). Supabase applies industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit.
  • Your browser (local storage): Your CV content, design choices, saved jobs, and wizard settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. This data does not leave your device unless you explicitly use a feature that sends it to our servers.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption for all connections and Row Level Security (RLS) on our database so users can only access their own records.

Section 05

Cookies and Local Storage

GamHub Jobs uses your browser's local storage (not traditional cookies) to save your work between sessions.

  • gamhubjobs_cv_data — your CV content
  • gamhubjobs_cv_wizard — your design choices
  • gamhubjobs_cv_theme — your selected CV theme
  • folio_saved_jobs — jobs you have saved
  • folio_cookie_consent — records whether you have accepted this notice

You can clear all locally stored data at any time through your browser's developer tools or settings.

Section 06

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — Database, authentication, and Edge Functions hosting. EU-based servers.
  • GamHubJobs.com — We display publicly available job listings sourced from GamHubJobs. We do not share your data with GamHubJobs.
  • Google Fonts — Typography loaded from Google's CDN. Google may log your IP address when fonts are loaded.
  • html2pdf.js / Cloudflare CDN — PDF generation library loaded from Cloudflare's CDN.
Section 07

Your Rights

  • Right to access: You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You can update or correct your information at any time by editing your CV or profile.
  • Right to erasure: You can request that we delete your account and all associated data. Email us at gamhubjobs@gmail.com.
  • Right to data portability: Your CV data is yours. You can download it as a PDF at any time.
  • Right to withdraw consent: You can delete your locally stored data through your browser settings at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at gamhubjobs@gmail.com. We will respond within 14 business days.

Section 08

Children's Privacy

GamHub Jobs is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at gamhubjobs@gmail.com and we will delete the information promptly.

Section 09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of GamHub Jobs after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
